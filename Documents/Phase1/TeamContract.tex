\section{How will we ensure that work will be shared fairly:}
Weekly updates of what we worked on that week
Set goals during weekly meetings

\section{Will you set internal deadlines?}
Yes, during each weekly meeting

\section{What will you do if a group member is not contributing:}
Sit down and discuss why they aren't contributing
Negotiate based off the situation

\section{What will happen if someone misses the deadline?}
We will prevent this from happening by constantly checking in with progress through Discord and Instagram
If someone does miss it, we will adjust to the situation and learn from it
