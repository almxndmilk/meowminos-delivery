\bigskip
{\centering \large{\textbf{Overview}}\\}

\paragraph{}
Meowmino’s Delivery is a 2D arcade style, grid-based game where the player controls a cat on a moped through a maze-like town. The objective is to collect all regular rewards (fish) scattered across the map and safely reach the mansion (exit) while avoiding enemies and managing score.

\paragraph{Inspiration Games:}Stardew Valley, Omori 

\paragraph{Backstory:}You're a Cat that lives in a pizza delivery store. You're a nice cat, and you want to share the happiness of your owner's pizza store. You steal your owner's moped and take off to deliver pizza to different animals in the neighbourhood (they promise fish in exchange). Be careful of the town’s doggy police! Since you’re a cat on a moped, don’t let them catch you or else they’ll steal your fish! Deliver pizza to the correct houses to gain extra fish. 

\bigskip
{\centering \large{\textbf{Game Structure \& Mechanics}}\\}
\smallskip

\paragraph{Win \& Lose Condition:}Collect all the regular fish found around the map. Reach and deliver pizza to the mansion at the end of the map to finish the game. Don't get caught by the dog police or go below 0 fish!

\paragraph{Board:}The game board is a town with roads the player moves around, with houses and trees acting as walls. You will start at the pizza shop, and end at the mansion across town.
The screen will display current fish count (score), and your elapsed playtime.

\paragraph{Barriers:}Some parts of the town can only be unlocked after a certain amount of deliveries. Continue delivering pizza until you reach the amount needed to enter each neighborhood.

\paragraph{Main Character:}A delivery cat on a moped that is controlled with W, A, S and D keys. Gain rewards by interacting with rewards on the ground (fish) and animals that want pizza(they give you fish), and increase your point count

\paragraph{\centering \large{\textbf{Enemies}}\\}~\\
Two types of enemies. Colliding with an enemy can cause loss of fish, and reduction of stats.

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Moving Enemies:} Police, follows main character within a certain range
	\item \textbf{Non-moving Enemies:} Puddles, temporary reduces movement speed 

\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Punishment:} If your score is below 0 fish, the game will be over

\bigskip
\paragraph{\centering \large{\textbf{Rewards}}\\}~\\
Points will be a fish counter at the top of the screen. Collect fish rewards to increase score.

\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Regular Rewards:} Static fish scattered around the map
	\item \textbf{Bonus Rewards:} Not every house wants pizza! Wait for the indicator to deliver pizza to the house

\end{itemize}
